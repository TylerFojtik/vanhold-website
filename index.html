Where others walk away

Some parcels come with a tangle: contaminated soil, a dozen owners, a derelict building, a number that does not pencil. We take that on. We handle demolition, environmental remediation, parcel assembly and financing gaps with foresight and creativity, and we work alongside the municipality rather than around it. What looks like a problem on paper is usually a place waiting for someone to finish it.

  • Environmental findings

    Phase II, remediation plan, VAP/NFA path and insurance priced into the deal.

  • Fragmented ownership

    Quiet assembly, options, and holdouts handled.

  • Obsolete structures

    Demolition and abatement sequenced with construction.

  • Zoning that doesn’t fit

    Entitlements, PUDs and variances.

  • Gap in the capital stack

    TIF, abatements, NMTC and public partnership.

  • Municipal priorities

    Plans that match the city’s land-use vision.

Process

From site to certificate of occupancy

01

Site selection & assembly

Step 1 of 6

  1. Site selection & assembly

    We look for the parcels others have written off: fragmented ownership, obsolete buildings, awkward access. We assemble them one owner at a time, under contract, before the market sees the whole picture.

    We handleParcel research · Owner outreach · Contracts · Assemblage

  2. Due diligence & environmental

    Before we close, we know what is in the ground and under the slab. Surveys, soils and Phase I and II work set the real cost of demolition and remediation.

    We handleTitle & survey · Phase I/II · Remediation scope · Demolition

  3. Entitlements & zoning

    We take plans through rezoning, variances and site-plan review with the municipality, not around it. Neighbors and officials hear the project from us early.

    We handleRezoning · Site plan approval · Community meetings · Permits

  4. Incentives & public partnership

    Where a site needs help to pencil, we negotiate it directly: TIF, abatements and shared infrastructure with the city. Summit Park, built beside Blue Ash's public investment, is the model.

    We handleTIF · Tax abatements · Development agreements · Public infrastructure

  5. Capital stack

    We structure the financing around the project, closing gaps that conventional lenders leave open. Equity, debt and public dollars are lined up before the first shovel.

    We handleEquity · Senior debt · Gap financing · Lender relations

  6. Development & construction management

    Our own team runs site development and vertical construction, so schedule and budget have one owner. We stay through lease-up and the certificate of occupancy.

    We handleSite development · Bidding · Project management · Closeout
